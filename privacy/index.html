3. How we obtained your business contact details

If you received a cold outreach email from us, your business contact details were obtained from one or more of the following publicly available or commercially licensed sources:

  • Apollo.io (B2B contact database)
  • Hunter.io or similar email-finding tools
  • Company websites and LinkedIn public profiles
  • Industry directories and public registers

We only hold your work email address and role-related information. We do not hold any personal data beyond what is necessary for one B2B outreach cycle.

4. How long we keep your data

Category Retention
Cold outreach contacts (no reply) Deleted or anonymised within 90 days of the last outbound touch

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with processors who help us operate:

  • Supabase Inc. (database hosting, EU/US) - data at rest in the EU region
  • Smartlead / email infrastructure providers - used to send cold outreach emails
  • Apollo.io - contact sourcing only; we do not transfer your data back to them
  • Cloudflare Inc. - CDN and DNS for omni-systems.ai

All processors are bound by data processing agreements. International transfers to the USA rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing while a dispute is resolved
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (this is the basis for cold outreach)
  • Portability - receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint with the Icelandic Data Protection Authority (personuvernd.is) or the supervisory authority in your country of residence

To exercise any of these rights, email thor@omni-systems.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

To unsubscribe from cold email immediately: reply to any email with "STOP" or "unsubscribe" and we will suppress your address within 24 hours and never contact you again.

7. Legitimate-interest basis for cold B2B outreach

We rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) to send one-off cold emails to business contacts whose role and company profile match organisations we help. We have conducted a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) that concludes:

  • Our interest: finding clients who could benefit from Omni Systems software
  • Necessity: cold B2B email is a proportionate way to reach decision-makers at target organisations
  • Balancing: we contact only corporate email addresses in roles where this communication is expected; we send a single initial email and a limited follow-up sequence; we provide a clear opt-out in every email; we do not contact individuals in Iceland (requires prior consent under Icelandic law)

You have the absolute right to object to this processing. One reply or click is all it takes and we will stop immediately and permanently.

8. Cookies and tracking

omni-systems.ai does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking pixels. We do not use open-tracking pixels in cold outreach emails. Server logs (IP, user-agent, timestamp) are retained for 30 days for security purposes only.

9. Changes to this notice

We will post any changes here with an updated effective date. Material changes that affect your rights will be communicated by email to active clients.
